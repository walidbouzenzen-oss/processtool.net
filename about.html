Our story

ProcessTool grew out of years of hands-on work on industrial projects, where the available hydraulic tools were either too complex, too expensive, or not built around the way engineers actually work. Flowmax was built to fill that gap: a focused, practical tool for the liquid, transient, gas and multiphase calculations engineers need, without unnecessary complexity.

Our mission

To give process and hydraulic engineers a reliable, straightforward calculation tool that fits into their project workflow, from early feasibility through to detailed design and documentation.

What guides us
