Questions

Before you buy.

How do I buy?

Request a quote with the edition and the number of users you need. We send you a quotation; when you accept it we invoice you, and the licence key and the download link follow.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, every edition, with the examples, the tutorials and the training manuals. Request it on the contact page.

Can I add a mode later?

Yes. Start with Liquid and add Transient or Gas & Multiphase when a project needs it; ask us for a quote.

What does the subscription include?

The program, every update during the year, and support by email; the editions with a second mode have priority support.

Do you sell project licences or to large teams?

Yes: a licence for the length of one project, and terms for larger teams. Ask us for a quote.

Which edition do I need?

Tell us about your networks and we will advise you; the free trial lets you check before you buy.

Not sure which edition is right for you?

Tell us about your networks and we will help you choose, or set up a free trial to see for yourself.
